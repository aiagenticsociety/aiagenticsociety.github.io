🏛️ Community Guidelines

Simple rules that keep this a high-signal, builder-first space. We're here to build — not to perform, promote, or pontificate.

The One Rule That Covers All Others
Respect the builder's time. Every post, comment, and interaction should make someone a better builder. If it doesn't — reconsider posting it.
How We Treat Each Other
🤝

Be respectful

Critique ideas, not people. Disagree with evidence, not attitude. All experience levels are welcome here.

🚫

No harassment or discrimination

Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind. First offense results in removal.

🔍

Assume good faith

Most confusion is accidental. Ask a clarifying question before escalating. Everyone here is trying to build something.

🪪

No impersonation or deception

Don't misrepresent your identity, credentials, or the results of your systems. Ship truth, not marketing.

What Belongs Here

✅ Post This

  • Workflow blueprints and working code
  • Failure post-mortems and lessons learned
  • Architecture diagrams and technical breakdowns
  • Tool comparisons backed by evidence
  • Questions — no such thing as too basic

🚫 Don't Post This

  • News links with no analysis or insight
  • Unsolicited product pitches or DM cold outreach
  • Benchmarks without methodology
  • Content teasers that gate the real value
  • Hype with no substance
Sharing Knowledge
📖

Open by default

Share fully. If you post a blueprint or approach, share it completely — no bait-and-switch to a newsletter or paid course.

🏷️

Credit your sources

If your work builds on someone else's, say so. Link the original. Tag the author. We grow by standing on each other's shoulders.

🔒

Respect confidential work

Don't share client data, employer code, or proprietary systems. Anonymise case studies where needed.

🏷️

Label what's untested

If you're theorising rather than reporting tested results, say so. Use [THEORY] or [UNTESTED] in your title.

AI Safety & Ethics
⚠️

No harmful agents

Don't share systems designed to deceive, surveil without consent, automate disinformation, or cause harm. This is non-negotiable.

🤖

Disclose AI-generated content

If your post was substantially AI-generated without your own review and validation, say so. Unverified AI output passed off as tested work wastes everyone's time.

🛡️

Include guardrails in blueprints

Any blueprint involving autonomous action — web calls, code execution, transactions — must include kill switches and human-in-the-loop checkpoints.

Reporting & Contact
🚩

Flag it

Use the report button on any post. Reviewed within 24 hours on weekdays, 48 hours on weekends.

📩

Contact a moderator

For sensitive issues, DM any mod directly or email aiagenticsociety@gmail.com. Your identity stays confidential.

🔒

Reporters are protected

Retaliation against anyone who files a good-faith report is itself a violation. You will never be punished for reporting something.
