🏛️ Community Guidelines & Rules

The operating principles that keep the AI Agentic Society a high-signal, builder-first space. Read them once. Internalize them always. We are here to build — not to perform.

Effective: March 2026
Applies to all members
v1.0.0 · ACTIVE
🎯

1. Our Mission & Who We Are

Why this community exists and what we stand for

▼

The AI Agentic Society is a builder-first community of professionals, developers, founders, and researchers who are serious about moving beyond AI hype into real-world production systems. We exist for one reason:

⬡
The era of "chatting" with AI is over. The era of building has begun. We architect multi-step agents, secure them with enterprise-grade guardrails, and share the blueprints that scale local experiments into global impact.

These guidelines exist to protect the signal-to-noise ratio of this community. Every rule below derives from one guiding principle: respect the builder's time.

💎

2. Core Values

The six principles that govern everything we do here

▼
🔨
Build Over Talk
Ship working systems, not opinions. Code, diagrams, and blueprints beat hot takes every time.
🔬
Radical Honesty
Share what failed, not just what worked. Post-mortems and failure files are as valuable as wins.
🛡️
Safety First
We never sacrifice ethics for speed. Responsible AI is non-negotiable, not optional.
🤝
Lift Others Up
Knowledge hoarding is the enemy. Share blueprints freely. Teach what you know.
📐
Precision Matters
Vague is useless. Be specific, cite sources, show your architecture, share your config.
🌐
Inclusive Excellence
The best builders come from everywhere. Gatekeeping kills innovation. Welcome all skill levels.
⚖️

3. Code of Conduct

Behavioural standards expected of every member

▼
R1
Be Respectful, Always
Treat every member — regardless of experience level, background, or viewpoint — with dignity. Disagreement is healthy; disrespect is not. Critique ideas, never people. No personal attacks, no condescension, no bullying.
MUST
R2
Zero Tolerance for Harassment
Harassment in any form — sexual, racial, political, or otherwise — results in immediate removal. This includes DMs. The community space extends to all interactions initiated through this society.
NEVER
R3
No Discrimination or Bigotry
Comments or content that discriminate based on gender, race, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or age are prohibited outright. The best code has no bias. Neither do we.
NEVER
R4
Assume Good Faith
Most confusion is intent, not malice. Before escalating, ask a clarifying question. Assume the other person is trying to build something useful, not destroy something valuable.
SHOULD
R5
No Impersonation or Deception
Do not impersonate other members, public figures, or AI systems. Do not misrepresent your credentials, affiliation, or the results of your systems. Ship truth, not marketing.
NEVER

Quick reference for everyday interactions:

✅ Do This

  • Give constructive, specific feedback on code or architectures
  • Share your failures alongside your wins
  • Credit others when building on their work
  • Welcome beginners with patience
  • Tag your posts with the right topic channels
  • Use threads to keep discussions organised

🚫 Don't Do This

  • Post "AI is going to take all our jobs" doom-scrolling content
  • Dismiss someone's question as "too basic"
  • DM members unsolicited to sell services
  • Repost others' blueprints without attribution
  • Argue about which LLM is "the best" without evidence
  • Flood channels with self-promotional content
📝

4. Content Standards

What belongs here, what doesn't, and how to post well

▼
💡
The signal bar is high. Before posting, ask: "Does this help someone build something better, faster, or safer?" If the answer is no, reconsider.
C1
Builder-First Content Only
All posts must be actionable for builders. Relevant content includes: workflow blueprints, architecture diagrams, code snippets, failure post-mortems, governance frameworks, tool comparisons with evidence, and research with practical implications.
MUST
C2
No Pure Hype or News Dumps
Sharing an article link with no context or opinion is not a post — it's a bookmark. Add your analysis, what it means for agentic workflows, and how builders should respond. News without insight is noise.
AVOID
C3
No Spam or Unsolicited Promotion
One self-promotional post per week max. Product launches, job postings, and service offers must be tagged #promo and posted only in designated channels. Cold outreach to members via DM is a bannable offense.
NEVER
C4
Cite Your Sources & Credit Collaborators
If your blueprint builds on someone else's work, say so. Link the original. Tag the author. This community grows by standing on each other's shoulders, not by stealing credit. Intellectual honesty is a hard requirement.
MUST
C5
Label Speculation Clearly
If you're theorising, predicting, or opining rather than reporting tested results, say so explicitly. Use markers like [THEORY], [UNTESTED], or [OPINION] in your post title so readers calibrate correctly.
SHOULD
C6
No Misleading Benchmarks
If you benchmark a model or system, share your full methodology, dataset, hardware spec, and evaluation criteria. Cherry-picked benchmarks erode trust. If your result can't be reproduced, it doesn't count.
NEVER
🔁

5. Knowledge Sharing Rules

How we share blueprints, code, and IP responsibly

▼
K1
Open by Default
Blueprints and code shared in community channels are assumed to be open for adaptation and iteration by other members, unless explicitly marked otherwise. Knowledge hoarding defeats the mission.
SHOULD
K2
Respect Proprietary Work
Do not share confidential employer data, client code, or proprietary systems without explicit permission. Anonymise case studies where needed. Real-world learnings are welcome; trade secrets are not yours to share.
MUST
K3
Document Your Failures
The Failure Files are a core pillar of this community. Post-mortems, failed experiments, and "what not to do" write-ups are actively encouraged and among the most valued contributions.
SHOULD
K4
No Gated Knowledge Drops
Do not post a teaser and then require members to subscribe to your newsletter, pay for a course, or DM you for the "real" content. If you're sharing, share fully. Bait-and-switch is banned.
NEVER
🛡️

6. AI Safety & Ethics Standards

Non-negotiable principles for responsible agentic development

▼
⚠️
This section has zero tolerance. Violations here result in immediate, permanent removal with no appeals process. We are building systems that act in the real world. The stakes are real.
S1
No Sharing of Harmful Agentic Systems
Do not share, promote, or celebrate agents designed to deceive, manipulate, surveil without consent, automate disinformation, execute malware, or cause demonstrable harm. This is not about being fragile — it's about being responsible engineers.
NEVER
S2
Disclose AI-Generated Content
If your post, blueprint, or case study was substantially generated by an AI model without human validation, say so. Do not present unreviewed AI output as your own tested, verified work. Hallucinated architectures cost real engineering time.
MUST
S3
Discuss Jailbreaks & Red-Teaming Responsibly
Security research, red-teaming, and adversarial prompt research are welcome — when framed as defensive learning. Do not post detailed jailbreak recipes or adversarial techniques without a clear defensive framing and appropriate warnings.
WARN
S4
Respect Data Privacy
Do not share personal data, user PII, or private communications — even in anonymised form — without appropriate consent. Your agent's logs are not community property unless you own the data and have legal right to share it.
MUST
S5
Include Guardrails in Blueprints
Any published workflow blueprint that involves autonomous action (web browsing, code execution, API calls, financial transactions) must include a guardrails section documenting rate limits, kill switches, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints.
MUST
⚡

7. Enforcement & Consequences

How violations are handled — transparently and consistently

▼

Moderators enforce these guidelines using a graduated response system. Severity is assessed based on intent, impact, and history. All moderation actions are logged internally.

Level Severity Trigger Examples Consequence
Level 1 ● LOW Off-topic post, missing tag, news dump without analysis, minor formatting issues Post removed with explanation. No strike issued. Educational DM from moderator.
Level 2 ● MEDIUM Spam, unsolicited promo, condescending tone, misleading benchmark, gated content bait Post removed. First strike issued. 7-day posting restriction. Warning on record.
Level 3 ● HIGH Harassment, discrimination, repeated violations, impersonation, sharing harmful systems Second strike. 30-day suspension. Appeal review required for reinstatement.
Level 4 ● PERMANENT Physical/sexual threats, sharing of harmful agents, data privacy violations, third strike Immediate permanent ban. No appeals. Reported to relevant platforms if applicable.
⚡
Moderators reserve the right to skip levels for severe first offenses. Context matters. Repeated bad-faith behavior accelerates the enforcement ladder. A "strike" expires after 180 days of clean record.
📣

8. Reporting a Violation

How to flag issues safely and what happens next

▼
📌
Use the Report Flag
Every post and message has a report button. Use it. Include a brief description of the violation. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours on weekdays, 48 hours on weekends.
📩
Direct Moderator Contact
For sensitive reports (harassment, data privacy, safety), DM any moderator directly or email mod@ai-agentic-society.dev. Your identity is kept confidential unless disclosure is legally required.
🔒
Reporter Protection
Retaliation against reporters is a Level 3 violation. You will never be punished for good-faith reporting, even if the reported content turns out not to be a violation. Report. We'll investigate.
⚖️
Appeals Process
Level 2 and Level 3 actions may be appealed within 14 days by emailing appeals@ai-agentic-society.dev. Appeals are reviewed by a panel of 3 senior moderators not involved in the original decision. Level 4 bans are not appealable.
🔄

9. Amendments & Versioning

How these guidelines evolve with the community

▼

These guidelines are a living document, versioned like software. Major changes go through community RFC (Request for Comment) before ratification.

A1
Quarterly Review Cycle
Guidelines are formally reviewed every quarter. Minor clarifications are merged without RFC. Any change to enforcement levels, new prohibited behaviours, or removal of protections requires a 7-day community RFC period.
A2
Member Proposals
Any member in good standing (no active strikes) may propose a guideline change by opening a Discussion tagged [RFC]. Proposals require 20 upvotes and moderator review to enter the formal amendment process.
A3
Change Log
All changes are documented in the CHANGELOG.md of the guidelines repository with version number, change summary, rationale, and effective date. Transparency is not optional.
